## Confirmed medications

### Fluticasone propionate 50 mcg nasal spray (generic Flonase)

- **Purpose:** Allergy symptoms — nasal congestion, sneezing, runny or itchy nose.
- **Form:** Nasal spray.
- **General OTC label information (NOT Shawn's prescription):** The OTC label's typical adult starting regimen is 2 sprays per nostril once daily, reduced to 1 spray per nostril once daily once symptoms are controlled.
- **⚠️ Shawn's actual prescribed regimen is NOT confirmed — confirm his dose before relying on this.**
- **Missed dose (general):** Take it when remembered. Skip it if it's nearly time for the next dose. Don't double up.
- **Storage:** Room temperature, keep the cap on. Discard per the label after the stated period once opened.
- **Common side effects:** Nosebleed, nasal irritation or dryness, headache, sore throat.
- **Get care if:** Severe nosebleeds, sores inside the nose that won't heal, eye pain or vision changes, signs of infection.
- **Onset note:** May take several days of daily use for full effect. Works best used regularly, not as-needed.
