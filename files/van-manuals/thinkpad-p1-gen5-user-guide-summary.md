## What the guide covers
- **Chapter 1 — Meet your computer:** front/side/bottom views, features and specifications, USB specifications.
- **Chapter 2 — Get started:** Wi-Fi, wired Ethernet, cellular (selected models), airplane mode; keyboard shortcuts, TrackPoint, trackpad, touch screen (selected models), external displays, Lenovo Precision Pen 2.
- **Chapter 3 — Explore your computer:** power management and battery behavior, Lenovo apps, and everyday features.
- **Chapter 4 — Secure your computer and information:** passwords (including UEFI/BIOS-level), fingerprint reader enrollment and associating fingerprints with power-on/hard-disk passwords, security chip / TPM, ThinkShutter, security-lock slot.
- **Chapter 5 — Configure advanced settings:** UEFI BIOS — entering the BIOS menu (F1 at the logo screen), boot priority order, OS Optimized Defaults, installing a new operating system (with the BitLocker/TPM precautions), BIOS updates and settings.
- **Chapter 6 — RAID:** storage configuration for the dual-M.2 models.
- **Chapter 7 — CRU replacement:** customer-replaceable units — procedures for the parts a user may swap (storage, memory, covers, etc.).
- **Chapter 8 — Help and support:** troubleshooting (including battery and power-button problems), Windows recovery options, Lenovo support resources.
- **Appendices:** compliance information, notices and trademarks.

## Key facts pulled for Scout
- **Enter UEFI BIOS:** restart, press **F1** at the logo screen. Save + exit with **F10**.
- **Specs (guide's table):** size 361.8 × 245.7 × ~18.4–18.7 mm; max heat output 135 W; AC adapter sine-wave input 100–240 V, 50–60 Hz; operating 5–35 °C, and the battery must be at least 10 °C to charge.
- **Dead-system reset (guide's troubleshooting):** if the system does not respond and will not power off, hold the power button 4+ seconds; if still stuck, remove AC power and use the emergency reset hole.
- **Battery troubleshooting themes:** over-discharged battery (charge, or use a quick charger if available), short runtime (full discharge/recharge cycle), won't charge when hot (remove, cool to room temperature, retry).
