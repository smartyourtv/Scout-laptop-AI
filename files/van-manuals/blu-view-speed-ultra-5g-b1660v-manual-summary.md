# BLU View Speed Ultra 5G (B1660V) — User Guide summary

**Source:** FCC-filed User Guide for B1660V (PDF in library: `blu-view-speed-ultra-5g-b1660v-user-guide.pdf`, 20 pages, ~1.1 MB, SHA-256 `51a33c972ba137846d31bf59fa19570c7c19085a041c04f9d623114e106603ca`).
**Note:** This is a paraphrased summary, not copied manual text. For exact wording, see the PDF. A close variant exists as B1660VL (Tracfone) — this guide is the B1660V (Verizon) version matching the van phone.

## The phone
- BLU View Speed Ultra 5G, model B1660V. Android 15 on the van phone.
- Layout: front camera, status bar, headset port, flash + rear cameras, volume keys, Power/Lock key with fingerprint sensor, USB port, back / recent-apps / home navigation.

## Setup
- SIM + microSD: use the included tray pin, slide tray out, place Nano SIM and microSD (not included) metal contacts down, slide back in. Keep SIM away from children; contacts damage easily.
- Charging: data cable to USB port on phone → power adapter → outlet. Charge fully before first use.
- Power on: long-press Power. Power off: press Power + Volume Up together, then tap Power Off on screen.
- Screenshot: press Power + Volume Down together.

## Everyday use (what the guide covers)
- Phone: dialer, favorites, recents, contacts, call history, call settings, Wi-Fi calling (needs Wi-Fi + carrier Wi-Fi calling service).
- Contacts: add/edit, save to Google / phone / SIM.
- Messages: text + multimedia, group chat, attach photos/video/sound/location, message settings.
- Email: Gmail setup, compose, attachments, settings.
- Chrome: browse, address/keyword search, bookmarks, settings.
- Bluetooth: pair via Settings → Connected devices, pair new device, use headset/car kit for calls/music.
- Wi-Fi: Settings → Network & internet → Internet → Wi-Fi, pick network, enter password if needed. Quick Settings (swipe status bar down twice) toggles Bluetooth/Wi-Fi.
- Camera: photo mode, video mode, gallery preview. Photos: view, edit, share.
- Calendar, Calculator, Clock (alarms, world clock), Google Maps, Google Play Store, Settings — standard Android flows, each with a short section.

## Safety (paraphrased)
- Not waterproof — keep away from water/liquids.
- Use only BLU-authorized batteries/accessories. Keep written backup of important info.
- Driving: follow traffic laws, both hands on wheel, do not text and drive.
- Hospital: follow rules, turn off near medical instruments. Airport/flight: follow regulations, do not use during flight.
- Do not use around fuels/chemicals or in explosion-hazard areas.
- Emergency calls: phone on + in service area, dial 911 from phone key.

## Troubleshooting
- Guide has a troubleshooting section (connectivity, calls, messages, internet/APN, voicemail) — general carrier-dependent checks: confirm service/plan, APN settings, network connection.

## Regulatory
- FCC ID: YHLBLUB1660V. SAR (as reported in guide): at ear 1.03 W/kg, body-worn 1.17 W/kg, against FCC limit 1.6 W/kg. Body-worn use tested with 1.0 cm separation, no-metal accessory.
