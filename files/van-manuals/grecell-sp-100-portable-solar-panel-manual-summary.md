# GRECELL SP-100 Portable Solar Panel — Manual Summary

**Source:** GRECELL SP-100 Portable Solar Panel User Manual (via manuals.plus). This file is a summary in my own words — not a copy of the manual.
**Applies to:** the van's portable panel — **GRECELL 100W Portable Foldable, 20V** (purchased Dec 1, 2022, Amazon).
**Match:** Model SP-100 in the manual matches the listing (100W, 20V max load voltage, foldable). This is an exact-model manual, not a family placeholder.

## Identification

- **Model:** SP-100.
- **Type:** Foldable portable solar panel, monocrystalline silicon cells.
- **Surface / fabric:** 840D Oxford fabric, PET frosted lamination.
- **Output interface:** MC4.

## Specs — SP-100 (from manual)

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Peak power | 100W |
| Solar energy conversion rate | 23% |
| Open-circuit voltage (Voc) | 23.85V |
| Max load voltage | 20V |
| Max load current | 5A |
| Operating temperature | -10°C to 60°C |
| Net weight | 4.67 kg ± 0.3 kg |
| Unfolded size | 1284 × 536 × 5 mm (50 × 21 × 0.2 in) |
| Folded size | 640 × 536 × 65 mm (25 × 21 × 2.5 in) |

**Van note:** 20V max load voltage means this panel must go through a charge controller (or a power station's solar input) — never directly to a 12V battery without regulation. The MC4 output is industry-standard; the included MC4-to-Anderson cable covers power stations that take Anderson input.

## What is in the box (per manual)

1 × 100W foldable panel, 1 × MC-4 to Anderson cable, 1 × MC-4 to DC 5.5×2.1mm cable, 1 × 5.5×2.1mm to 5.5×2.5mm adapter, 1 × 5.5×2.1mm to 7.9×0.9mm adapter, 1 × 5.5×2.1mm to 3.5×1.5mm adapter, 1 × user manual.

## Use — key points (paraphrased)

- Read the manual before use; keep it for reference.
- Unfold fully and face toward sun; keep the whole panel uncovered — covering part of a panel stops charging.
- Works in cloudy conditions (reduced output) — keep it out even without direct sun.
- Keep the power station / battery in shade while the panel sits in sun, to avoid overheating the battery side.
- Keep panel clean — brush off dust, wipe with a damp (not wet) cloth; remove sap/residue promptly.
- Do not bend more than ~30° — bending further risks breaking it.
- Keep away from fire/heaters, strong water jets, submersion, and sharp objects. Do not disassemble, puncture, or step on it.
- Do not charge a car battery directly with the panel (manual's restriction — use a controller / power station input).
