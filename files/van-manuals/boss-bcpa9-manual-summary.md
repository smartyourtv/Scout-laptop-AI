## The unit
- BOSS Audio Systems BCPA9 — digital media AM/FM receiver, single-DIN chassis with Double-DIN conversion brackets, detachable/floating 9-inch capacitive touchscreen.
- The van's dash stereo. Wireless Apple CarPlay + Android Auto, Bluetooth hands-free + streaming, no DVD/CD.
- Support (from manual family): BOSS Audio Systems, 3451 Lunar Court, Oxnard, CA 93030; customer service 805-751-4853; tech support www.bossaudio.com/support.

## Front / rear detail (paraphrased)
- Front: MENU (home/previous source), voice-assistant button, power/volume knob (long-press = on/off, short press = mute, rotate = volume), MODE, BAND (FM/AM), reset pinhole (bottom).
- Rear: microphone input, front camera input, rear camera input, USB 1 + USB 2, main harness, pre-amp outputs, A/V input, AM/FM antenna.

## Installation — key points
- Sliding metal housing (anti-theft); make all power/speaker/antenna connections before sliding the chassis in; a metal strap can fix the unit to the car's lower bracket; the supplied keys slide the chassis back out.
- Professional installation recommended (manual's own warning). Do not watch video while driving.
- Screen: tilts ~25° forward/back, ~15° left/right, height adjustable; detach by holding both release buttons and pulling the panel forward.
- Screen care: clean only when off/cool/out of direct sun, dry microfiber cloth; no ammonia/window cleaner/abrasives; clean dry fingers — moisture affects the capacitive touch.

## Operation (what the manual covers)
- First-time setup: date/time via CarPlay phone sync, RDS/RBDS auto-set, or manual; language English/Spanish (Settings → General → Language); RDS/RBDS options (RT data, TA, AF) under Settings → Radio.
- Radio: tuning, presets, band switching.
- CarPlay / Android Auto: wired via USB Port 1 or wireless (Bluetooth + Wi-Fi on the phone); Siri / "Ok Google" voice control; rearrange CarPlay apps from the iPhone.
- Bluetooth: first-time pairing — search "BOSS AUDIO BCPA9" on the phone, confirm the passcode shown on both; auto-connect on, auto-answer off by default; phonebook + call-history download; dial pad, contacts/history search, private mode; audio streaming with track control (device volume at max, control volume on the unit; album art not supported over Bluetooth).
- USB media playback, camera view (auto-switches in reverse, volume lowers), audio settings (EQ, sub level/LPF), general settings.
- Troubleshooting table in the manual: unit unresponsive → press RESET on the front panel; no power → fuse/wiring/ignition; no audio → speaker connections/volume/source; Bluetooth issues → re-pair; CarPlay/Android Auto not launching → phone OS, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth, restart both.
