## Identification

- **Model:** EFR182
- **Brand:** Frigidaire — manufacturer Curtis International Ltd.
- **Capacity:** 1.6 cu ft (1.8 cu ft listed in some specifications — the manual's own table notes both)
- **Configuration:** compact, single door, internal freezer (half-width ice cube chamber)
- **Color:** stainless steel
- **Voltage:** 120V
- **Weight:** 28 lbs (12.7 kg)
- **Refrigerant:** R600a (flammable — see safety)
- **Defrost:** automatic for the main compartment; the ice-cube chamber needs manual defrosting when ice builds up
- **Features:** adjustable leveling legs, flat-back design, reversible door, removable/slide-out shelf, 2L door basket, tall bottle holder
- **Dimensions:** the manual's diagram gives H 19.8 × D 16.73 × W 17.83 in; its spec table lists 18.5 × 20 × 18.5 in (D×W×H). The two figures disagree — measure the unit itself before planning a tight fit.
- **Warranty:** 1 year from purchase (manufacturing defects / workmanship, normal household use). Keep the proof of purchase.

## Safety

- Household use only. Do not store explosive substances (e.g., aerosol cans with flammable propellant) inside.
- Keep the appliance properly grounded; keep ventilation openings clear.
- Do not damage the refrigerant circuit. R600a is flammable: no mechanical devices or sharp instruments to speed up defrosting; service by trained personnel only.
- Unplug before cleaning or maintenance.
- If discarding the unit, remove the doors first (child entrapment risk) and dispose of it per local regulations for flammable-refrigerant appliances.

## Setup

- **Settle first:** after unpacking, let the fridge stand upright 2–4 hours before plugging in so refrigerant fluids settle.
- **Placement:** firm, level surface, away from direct sunlight and heat sources (stoves, heaters). Allow air circulation around the unit. Level with the front leveling legs.
- **Power:** dedicated, grounded 120V AC outlet. No extension cords or adapter plugs. Keep the cord unpinched and undamaged.
- **Reversible door:** the door can be swapped to open from the other side by moving the hinges to the opposite side (diagrams in the full manual).

## Operation

- **Temperature dial:** mechanical dial inside the unit. Higher number = colder. Range roughly −2 °C to 2 °C (32–35.6 °F). Allow several hours for the temperature to stabilize after adjusting.
- **Storage:** 1.6 cu ft suits beverages and snacks. Use the slide-out shelf for organization; the ice-cube chamber makes ice or holds small frozen items; the door's tall bottle holder fits larger bottles.

## Care and maintenance

- **Cleaning:** unplug first. Interior/exterior with a soft cloth and mild detergent; baking-soda-and-warm-water solution for the interior. No abrasive cleaners or harsh chemicals. Clean the door gasket regularly for a good seal.
- **Defrosting the freezer chamber:** unplug, remove contents, let ice melt naturally. Never use sharp objects to chip ice off.
- **Sounds:** gurgling/boiling sounds from refrigerant circulation and thermostat clicks are normal.

## Troubleshooting (paraphrased)

- **Does not run:** check the plug, the circuit breaker, and that the dial is not set to OFF.
- **Not cold enough:** door opened too often; warm food put in; poor air circulation / blocked clearance; dial set too warm.
- **Unusual noises:** unit not level (adjust legs); something vibrating against it; some compressor sounds are normal.
- **Door won't close:** unit not level; gasket dirty or damaged; contents blocking closure.
