## Placement and installation

- Level, stable surface with room for intake/outlet airflow. The manual's placement diagram shows 12 in clearance above and 3 in at the sides and back.
- Grounded outlet; do not defeat the grounding. Radio/TV interference: move the oven, reorient the antenna, or use a different circuit.
- **Turntable:** the glass tray and roller ring must always be in place during cooking. Never place the glass tray upside down; never restrict the tray or ring. If the tray or ring cracks or breaks, get it replaced before using the oven.

## Control panel

- **CLOCK** — set the clock (12- or 24-hour, toggled with the CLOCK pad)
- **TIME COOK** — timed cooking
- **TIME DEFROST** — defrost by time
- **WEIGHT DEFROST** — defrost by weight
- **POWER LEVEL** — set/check cooking power
- **KITCHEN TIMER** — countdown timer
- **Number pads 0–9** — time, weight, counts
- **STOP/CLEAR** — pause/cancel, and control-panel lock
- **0/UNLOCK** — unlock the door (also number pad 0)
- **START/+30 SEC.** — start, and add 30 seconds
- **EXPRESS COOK** — quick programs
- **AUTO COOK MENUS** — POTATO, POPCORN, PIZZA, BEVERAGE, FROZEN DINNER, REHEAT

## Clock and kitchen timer

- **Clock:** in standby, press CLOCK once for 12-hour or again for 24-hour. Enter the time with the number pads, confirm with CLOCK or START/+30 SEC. During cooking, the current time can be checked with the CLOCK pad.
- **Kitchen timer:** press KITCHEN TIMER, enter the countdown (up to 99 min 99 s), press START/+30 SEC. to start. Check the countdown with KITCHEN TIMER; STOP/CLEAR cancels it. The buzzer beeps when it ends.

## Child safety door lock — READ THIS FIRST (it locks the door by itself)

This oven locks its own door — it is not a malfunction.

- The door **locks automatically and immediately** when a cooking program starts.
- **During cooking:** press 0/UNLOCK or STOP/CLEAR once to unlock if you need to open the door; to resume, press START/+30 SEC. If cooking is not resumed after closing the door, the door locks again within 10 seconds.
- **When cooking ends:** the display alternates End/Loc and the buzzer beeps every 2 minutes for 30 minutes. The door stays locked for 30 minutes unless you unlock it (0/UNLOCK or STOP/CLEAR once). After 30 minutes it unlocks and returns to standby.
- **To deactivate the lock entirely:** in standby, hold 0/UNLOCK for 3 seconds (display shows Loc), then press 7, then 9 — display shows ULoc, buzzer beeps once. The door can now be opened any time.
- **To reactivate:** same steps from ULoc back to Loc. The setting survives a power outage.
- **Control panel lock (separate):** hold STOP/CLEAR for 3 seconds to disable all pads; hold again 3 seconds to re-enable.

## Cooking

- **Time cook:** TIME COOK → enter time (max 99 min 99 s) → POWER LEVEL (default P100) → START/+30 SEC. Power levels: P100 = 700W, then P-90 = 630W, P-80 = 560W, P-70 = 490W, P-60 = 420W, P-50 = 350W, P-40 = 280W, P-30 = 210W, P-20 = 140W, P-10 = 70W, P-00 = 0W. During cooking, POWER LEVEL shows the current level; START/+30 SEC. adds time.
- **Quick start:** in standby, press START/+30 SEC. repeatedly — each press adds 30 seconds at full power (max 99:99).
- **Express cook:** in standby, press EXPRESS COOK pads 1–6 for 1–6 minutes at full power, starting automatically.
- **Time defrost:** TIME DEFROST → enter time (max 99:99) → START. The oven pauses and beeps partway to remind you to turn the food over; close the door and press START to continue.
- **Weight defrost:** WEIGHT DEFROST → enter weight (1–96 oz) → START. Time and power are set automatically; same pause-and-turn behavior.
- **Multistage cooking:** up to 3 automatic programs — time defrost plus one or two microwave programs, or two microwave programs at different temperatures. Time defrost can only be the first stage.
- **Auto cook menus** (press the pad, enter the count, press START):
  - Potato: 4–6 oz per serving — press POTATO once; 8–10 oz — press twice. Count 1–4 potatoes.
  - Popcorn: press POPCORN once — sets the time for a 3.5 oz bag. Fold the bag's four corners and center it.
  - Pizza: one ~5 oz slice — press PIZZA once; a whole ~16 oz frozen pizza — press twice.
  - Beverage: 5–7 oz cups — press BEVERAGE once; 9–11 oz cups — press twice. Count 1–4 cups.
  - Frozen dinner: 7–9 oz entrees — press FROZEN DINNER once; 10–12 oz — press twice. Press 1 for one entree, 2 for two.
  - Reheat: one plate of leftovers, 12–18 oz per serving — press REHEAT once.

## Cooking techniques

- **Stirring:** stir casseroles and vegetables partway so heat spreads evenly (edges absorb more energy than centers).
- **Arrangement:** put thicker, meatier parts toward the outside of the turntable, delicate parts (asparagus tips) toward the center.
- **Shielding:** cover narrow areas (poultry wing tips, ends of legs, corners of square dishes) with small strips of aluminum foil to prevent overcooking. Small amounts only — large amounts can damage the oven.
- **Turning:** turn large foods (roasts) over midway so all sides get energy.
- **Standing:** let foods stand a few minutes after heating so internal heat evens out (especially roasts, whole vegetables, cakes).
- **Adding moisture:** a little water on dry food helps it cook.

## If the door won't open

1. Press 0/UNLOCK or STOP/CLEAR once — one beep and a click means it unlocked.
2. Two beeps means press 0/UNLOCK or STOP/CLEAR again quickly (within 3 seconds).
3. Still stuck: unplug the oven, then plug it back in.
- **Light on + turntable turning after pressing the door button:** normal, no heat output, no risk. Press 0/UNLOCK or STOP/CLEAR, then the door button — the door opens, the light comes on, and the turntable stops.

## If the oven does not work

1. Confirm it is firmly plugged in.
2. Plug another appliance into the same outlet to check the outlet.
3. Confirm the door is fully closed.
4. If it still does not work, call for service.
- Before calling: check the plug is secure, wait 10 seconds and re-plug, test the outlet with another appliance, confirm the door is closed, the panel is programmed correctly, and the timer is set.

## Cleaning and care

- Unplug before cleaning.
- Interior: damp cloth; mild detergent for heavy soil. No spray or harsh cleaners (they can stain, streak, or dull surfaces).
- Exterior: damp cloth. Do not let water seep into the ventilation openings.
- Window: damp cloth, both sides.
- Control panel: soft damp cloth only — do not let it get wet. Leave the door open while cleaning the panel so the oven cannot start accidentally.
- Steam inside/around the door in humid conditions is normal — wipe with a soft cloth.
- Glass tray: remove and wash in warm soapy water or the dishwasher.
- Roller ring + oven floor: clean regularly (noise); wipe the floor with mild detergent; the ring can be washed in soapy water or the dishwasher. Reinstall the ring in its proper position.
- Odors: a cup of water with the juice and skin of one lemon in a deep microwaveable bowl, microwaved 5 minutes, then wipe and dry.
- Bulb replacement: by a certified electrician.
- Keep the oven clean and free of food deposits — neglect can deteriorate surfaces and create a hazard.
- Disposal: do not put it in normal trash; take it to a recycling center.
